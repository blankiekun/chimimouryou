## The social hub and Community

When you use the hub:

- Keep your password and recovery code safe; there is no email to recover an account.
- Don't post illegal content, harass or threaten anyone, spam, or impersonate others.
- Don't post whole books or long parts of them. A Community post quotes a line and a short

- Don't try to break, overload or get around the hub's limits.

Moderators can remove posts, names, pictures and covers, and remove accounts that break these
rules. The hub is run on a best-effort basis: it can change, be limited or stop at any time,
with or without notice, and data on it can be lost.
